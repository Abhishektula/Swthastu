# Swthastu website review — 19 October 2026

Scope: canonical Sites source, public copy, data capture and service representations. Reviewed home, journeys, retreats, coaches, workshops/rituals and five emotional-wellbeing programmes, calendar, Rishi, My Space, provider registration, partners, about and privacy; added At Work, EAP and Care Standards. The separate www.swthastu.com domain could not be independently retrieved. This is a content and implementation review, not legal certification, a penetration test or an audit of actual service delivery. Browser visual QA was unavailable in this environment.

## Changes
- Added homepage workplace segment, navigation, six service streams, EAP journey, employer responsibilities, recommended practices, programme design and organisation-only scoping form.
- Distinguished applicable duties from optional EAP practice. No universal central requirement to purchase EAP identified; applicability needs establishment-specific advice.
- No invented clinical roster, 24/7 cover, guaranteed outcomes or government approval. Workplace services are proposed for scoping until contracted.
- Added care standards on Ayurveda, clinical scope, foods, advertising, AI illustrations, booking terms and optional spiritual participation.
- Added adult confirmation to enquiries; employer payload uses a strict allowlist and requires explicit consent. No clinical narrative field in employer form. Records carry consent version and timestamp.
- Provider applications request professional registration/scope information; submission is not credential verification or automatic publication.
- Clarified Rishi is not EAP counselling, clinical assessment or an employment-decision tool.
- Updated privacy wording for phased DPDP commencement; no unsupported certification or blanket deletion promise.
- Past proposed calendar dates now invite enquiries for future dates instead of implying availability.

## Operator actions still required
Before clinical/EAP activation: confirm legal entity/address and designated grievance contact; verify qualifications and relevant registrations; contract care providers, clinical responsibility, eligibility, hours, escalation and private access channels. Agree processor terms, retention/deletion schedules, access controls, breach response and aggregate reporting with small-group suppression. Do not route clinical records through the public employer form.

Check actual service locations, state rules, occupational-health duties, professional indemnity and facility permissions. Obtain applicable licences/labels if selling medicines, regulated foods or clinical services. Publish accurate fees, cancellation/refund terms and service-provider identity before accepting payment. Obtain qualified legal advice for Indian and overseas operations; this review cannot establish global compliance.

The public site deliberately discloses these service limits. Website edits do not substitute for implementing these operational controls.
